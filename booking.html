Booking

Grooming is by appointment, daycare is booked by phone. Here is exactly how it works.

Grooming appointments

Grooming services are by appointment only. Call us and we will match your dog with a dedicated groomer and a time that keeps their stay as short as possible.

Grooming service prices are subject to change due to your pet’s weight, size and hair length during our intake evaluation.

All grooming dogs must be up to date on vaccinations and must show proof of rabies.

Call (508) 545-1046

Daycare bookings

To book a daycare spot for your adorable companion, please give us a bark at (508) 545-1046. Our friendly team is ready to help you schedule a day filled with play, pampering and all-around happiness for your pet.

Before a first visit we run a behavioural evaluation ($25) so we understand your dog’s temperament around both people and dogs.

Important: confirming your grooming booking

Please be cautious of any communication claiming to represent Mr. Nice Dog outside of our official channels. To ensure the authenticity of your grooming appointment, kindly verify directly with our team on (508) 545-1046.

About the card pre-authorisation

In order to save your credit card information for payment after your grooming service is completed, we run a pre-authorisation charge on your card. This charge is solely for verifying your card information and will not be the final charge for your grooming service. Once the service is completed we will charge the full amount to your saved card. If you have any questions about this process, please don’t hesitate to contact us.
