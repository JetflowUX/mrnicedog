Dog Daycare in Natick, MA

An indoor dog park, a designated attendant on duty, and open beds instead of crates.

Daycare at Mr. Nice Dog

For pet parents needing daycare, we are happy to offer this service during our business hours — a morning, an afternoon, or a full day.

Our facility has a state-of-the-art indoor play “park” with an automatic cleaning system to keep areas tidy and clean. There is always a designated daycare attendant on duty who monitors and watches over all the dogs in attendance.

Daycare involves a combination of play time, socialisation and relaxation periods with the other dogs attending. We do not use holding crates in our facility — we use individual open beds for your dog’s comfort.

Pet parents are encouraged to bring snacks and food if needed. We offer snacks at no cost, though there is an additional fee for any food we provide. You must inform us of your dog’s food-related restrictions, sensitivities and allergies.
