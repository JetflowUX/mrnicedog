Dog Grooming in Natick, MA

Baths, breed-specific clips, hand-stripping and full spa services — in a salon that never uses crates.

What makes us different

Our grooming is based on a holistic approach, to create a stress-free environment for your dog. The mental and social wellbeing of your pet is our top priority.

We do not have or use crates in our salon, and we schedule appointments to minimise the time your dog spends here. We use the finest shampoos, conditioners and products — gentle, hypoallergenic and suited to all skin types: normal, dry and oily.

When checking in, we analyse your dog’s skin and coat and talk through any concerns. Dogs sometimes arrive unexpectedly matted; when that happens we may have to focus the day’s service on removing the mats before anything else.

Before you book: all pets must be up to date on their rabies vaccination. When booking a service, please attach your pet’s vaccination certificate.

Our grooming services

Choose the level of service your dog needs, from a refreshing brushout to a full clip.

A full body brush using dry shampoo and conditioner.

A thorough deep cleaning bath using tailored shampoos and conditioners with a thorough dry.

Specific to terrier breeds, hand-stripping removes hair from the dog’s undercoat and dead hair from the topcoat. It is an involved longer process that requires the groomer to use specific tools and work meticulously for the best results.

Similar to the “Nice” Bath with the addition of a nail clip, pad trim, ear and teeth cleaning, sanitary trim and eyes trim.

Similar to the “Deluxe Nice” Bath with a breed specific haircut or your haircut preferences.

A series of 4 visits to introduce your puppy into the world of grooming. The visits gradually expose your puppy to our full range of services so that they become more comfortable each time — a perfect way to begin a lifetime of good dog hygiene.
