Frequently asked

A lot depends on the pet’s lifestyle and living conditions. As a general guide, dogs who have their hair cut in particular clips — Poodles, Cockers, Lhasa Apsos, Shih Tzus, Bichons and so on — should be groomed every 4–6 weeks. Bath dogs such as Labradors, Shepherds and Collies should be groomed every 6–8 weeks.

When you arrange an appointment with us you are provided with a dedicated pet groomer who will discuss your dog’s grooming requirements. In over 95% of cases we complete the grooming process in 2 hours or less, giving your pet a positive experience in a modern and spacious facility.

Some clients prefer to keep the same groomer. If your dog is nervous, or you have specific requests, we encourage requesting the same groomer because it fosters a relationship between the client, the groomer and the dog.
