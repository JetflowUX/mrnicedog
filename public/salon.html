Our salon

The new concept for your best friend has arrived in Natick — bright, climate-controlled and crate-free.

Built around the dog, not the schedule

A climate-controlled facility with state-of-the-art equipment, bright colours and a lot of room to move.

Grooming stations sit along one wall, the bathing room is fitted with stainless steel tubs, and the indoor park runs the length of the building behind white picket fencing. Dogs rest in individual open beds. There is not a crate in the building.

  • Climate-controlled throughout
  • Indoor play park with an automatic cleaning system
  • Stainless steel bathing suite
  • Fully insured

Come and take a tour

Walk in during opening hours, meet the team and see the salon for yourself. No appointment needed just to look around.
