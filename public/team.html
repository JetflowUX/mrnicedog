One dedicated groomer per dog

When you arrange an appointment you are provided with a dedicated pet groomer who will discuss your dog’s grooming requirements.

If your dog is nervous, or you have specific requests, we encourage requesting the same groomer each visit — it fosters a relationship between the client, the groomer and the dog.
